On the next page, you will use an AI chatbot with a chat panel on the left and an AI assumption scores panel on the right. These scores are the model’s best guess about what it thinks you need from the conversation (for example, how much you want validation vs. objective input, or what kind of support you may be seeking). 

The right panel has two sections (Section 1 and Section 2) with 6 scores in total. Section 2 appears lower on the page. Scroll the right panel to see it.

How you will use the tool?

  1. You will discuss the situation you wrote about earlier with the AI chatbot.
  2. Please complete at least 8 turns.
  3. After each AI response, review each AI assumption score on the right panel by giving it a 👍 (agree) or 👎 (disagree).
  4. If you disagree with a score, give it a 👎. Then drag the slider to match your own view as closely as possible, click Confirm new score, and briefly explain your rationale in the text box.
  5. You must review all scores in section 1 and section 2 (6 scores in total) before you can send your next message. Section 2 is below section 1 — scroll down on the right panel to reach it. We know this takes attention and effort, and we truly appreciate your careful review.
  6. The chart at the top of each section shows how the AI's assumed scores (and your adjustments) evolve across conversational turns.
  7. When you are done with the conversation, click Complete Chat to finish.
 
These instructions will be available on the interface.
